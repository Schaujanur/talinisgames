The case

A radio message reaches the airship mid‑voyage: there is a saboteur aboard. Find out who it is before landing, or the ship goes up in flames.

Question passengers and crew, search the decks, compare what people tell you. Every step costs time aboard, so choose where to look.

No two cases are alike. The culprit, the motive, who had access and who is lying are generated anew for every voyage, and every case is built to be solvable from the clues you can find.
