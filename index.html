Prologue

The studio

Talinis Games is a small independent studio named after Mount Talinis, the twin peaks above Valencia in Negros Oriental.

We make games and apps we would want to play ourselves.

Three worlds

Every game and app gets a world of its own.

Three doors lead out of the rainforest: into the Elbe valley of the sixteenth century, into the year 1936, and down to the sea off Negros.
