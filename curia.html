Incipit

A settlement on the Elbe

Roads, goods chains, families and the turning seasons shape your settlement.

From a farmstead in a side valley it grows into a hamlet, a market town and finally a chartered town — or it visibly fails. Bottlenecks show on the map, not in a quest list: goods wait by the road, a building site stalls, a road is missing. Your residents live, work and grow old across three generations.
